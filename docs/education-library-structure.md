# THARi BOOKS — Education Library Structure (Planning Note)

> Status: planning reference only. This file does not contain official textbook content and is not a substitute for the latest Ministry of Education syllabus.

## 1. Confirmed grade structure

Use the KG+12 structure:

- Kindergarten: KG
- Primary: Grades 1–5
- Middle: Grades 6–9
- High school: Grades 10–12

The 5+4+3 division is described in Myanmar Digital News' explanation of the KG+12 curriculum. Grade names and groups should remain separate from the older pre-KG+12 grade numbering.

## 2. Grade and subject catalog approach

Create one catalog entry for every grade. Do not assume that every subject is offered in every grade.

Each grade's subject list must be checked against the current official syllabus or official textbook/teacher-guide publication before it is marked as confirmed.

Known official reference points found during initial research:

- Department of Myanmar Examinations lists KG textbook and teacher-guide resources.
- The Department's Grade 1 new-curriculum textbook notice lists Myanmar, English and Mathematics.
- A Ministry of Education secondary textbook/teacher-guide development guideline (September 2017) lists indicative subject families for Grades 6–12. It is a historical planning document, so its subject/page table must not be treated as the current final syllabus.

## 3. Suggested subject record fields

Every subject record should contain:

- Grade
- Official subject name (Myanmar)
- Official subject name (English), if available
- Curriculum / syllabus version or academic year
- Source URL and source publisher
- Verification status: `unverified`, `source-found`, `verified-current`
- Available learning resources
- Rights / license status

## 4. Learning-resource structure

Within a grade and subject, organize original THARi learning content as:

1. Subject overview and learning goals
2. Chapter list
3. Lesson explanation in clear Myanmar language
4. Worked examples
5. Key terms / Myanmar-English glossary
6. Original diagrams or learning visuals
7. Practice questions (easy → challenging)
8. Answer explanations
9. Chapter summary and revision
10. Exam-practice sets, where appropriate

Teacher-facing resources may be a separate collection and should not be mixed with student resources.

## 5. Content and rights rules

- Do not upload or sell copied official textbook pages, scans, images, or teacher-guide text unless the relevant permission or license is confirmed.
- Prefer links to official public resources where linking is permitted.
- THARi-created explanations, examples, diagrams, exercises and answer explanations must be original and reviewed for accuracy.
- Record source references, authorship, review date and permission/license status for every resource.
- Do not label a resource as an official textbook unless it is an authorized official publication.
