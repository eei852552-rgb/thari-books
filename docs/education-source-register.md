# THARi BOOKS — Education Source Register

Last checked: 2026-11-14

This register separates what the available sources actually confirm from what still needs checking. It is a research aid, not an official syllabus.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current Ministry of Education / Department of Myanmar Examinations publications and official resource pages.
2. Official curriculum-framework documents with a clearly stated version.
3. Reputable education platforms only as discovery aids; verify their claims against official publications before publishing.
4. Older news articles, reposts and third-party file mirrors are historical clues only.

## Confirmed from official Department of Myanmar Examinations pages

### Kindergarten (KG)

The Department's "New Curriculum KG Text Book and Teacher Guide" page lists these resources:

- KG-1 Language Guide
- KG-2 Learning Guide
- KG-3 Teacher Guide

These are listed as KG learning/teacher-guide resources. Do not automatically present all three as student textbooks.

Source: https://www.myanmarexam.org/news/23

### Grade 1

The Department's Grade 1 new-curriculum notice surfaced in search results lists three core resources: Myanmar Reader, English and Mathematics. A separate official Department page specifically titled "KG+12 New Curriculum Textbooks (Grade 1)" lists six downloadable textbook subjects:

- မြန်မာဖတ်စာ (Myanmar Reader)
- အင်္ဂလိပ်စာ (English)
- သင်္ချာ (Mathematics)
- သိပ္ပံ (Science)
- လူမှုရေး (Social Studies)
- စာရိတ္တနှင့်ပြည်သူ့နီတိ (Moral and Civics)

For the Grade 1 catalogue, use the six-item textbook page as the stronger resource-specific evidence, while preserving the discrepancy in the source notes. Confirm the current edition/year and reuse permissions before linking or publishing.

Sources:
- Notice: https://www.myanmarexam.org/news2679.html?page=1
- Resource-specific textbook page reported in the initial search: https://myanmarexam.org/news/34 (direct retrieval returned 404 during the latest check; locate a working official URL before adding it as a public catalogue link).

## Additional government-published primary-grade subject evidence

A government social-education information page published by the President's Office site lists school materials provided for Grades 1–5. Its listed learning subjects include:

- Grades 1–3: Myanmar, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, and Moral and Civics.
- Grades 4–5: the above subjects plus Life Skills, Physical Education, and Arts (Painting and Music).

Important limitation: the page is a school-material support / provision list, not a grade-by-grade current curriculum syllabus or a complete official textbook catalogue. It is useful corroborating evidence for subject families in primary grades, but THARi BOOKS must still verify exact subject titles, grade applicability, textbook availability, and academic-year version against current Ministry of Education publications before marking individual subject records as verified-current.

Source: https://presoffministry.gov.mm/my/news-social-education?page=6

## Official Ministry of Education learning resources and recent curriculum signals

The Ministry's official radio-lesson archive contains Grade 6 learning materials / introductions for Geography, Science, English and Mathematics, as well as other Grade 6 learning items. These pages show that subject-specific Grade 6 learning resources exist, but the archive is not a complete or necessarily current textbook syllabus. Treat it as resource discovery evidence, not a full subject list.

Sources:
- https://www.moe.gov.mm/my/radio-lessons

- https://moe.gov.mm/my/node/2465

A Ministry of Education news item published in 2026 says curricula for industrial, agriculture and livestock subjects were being revised for teaching Grades 6–9 from the next June school-opening period. This is a policy-development signal, not proof that these subjects have already been implemented in every school or grade. Recheck the current implementation status and official syllabus before adding them to the catalogue.

Source: https://moe.gov.mm/en/node/5400

## Ministry radio lessons: additional grade-specific learning evidence

The Ministry of Education's official radio-lesson archive includes these grade-labelled items:

- Grade 1: English introduction and Myanmar poem lesson.
- Grade 2: Life Skills lesson about local foods; English lesson "Family weekend"; Mathematics / measurement lesson "Which is heavier?"
- Grade 3: Science lesson on floating and sinking; Mathematics lesson on time and duration; Myanmar curriculum introduction; Arts / Life Skills and other individual lessons.
- Grade 4: Social Studies introduction.
- Grade 6: Geography, Science, Mathematics and Myanmar subject lessons / introductions.
- Grade 7: History lesson on Marxism.
- Grade 10: Mathematics and Chemistry lessons.

These are individual teaching / audio resources, not complete textbook lists or full grade syllabuses. They establish only that the Ministry archive contains learning items carrying those grade labels. Do not infer the full subject catalogue from this archive.

The Ministry also reported in October 2023 that the KG+12 curriculum had been implemented across all grades in the 2023–2024 academic year. This confirms the rollout milestone at that time, but does not establish that every textbook edition or subject list remains unchanged today.

A Ministry annual-report document also states that textbook revision/printing work for Grades 2–5 began in fiscal year 2019–2020, middle-school textbook work in 2020–2021, and high-school textbook work in 2021–2022. It names Myanmar, English, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies as basic-education textbooks in the 2019–2020 section. This is historical programme evidence, not a current grade-by-grade catalogue, and the document does not establish that every named subject applies identically to every grade.

## Official textbook-development evidence for middle and upper grades

The Ministry's education-reform presentation documents that new-curriculum Grade 6 printed textbooks were prepared for use in the 2019–2020 academic year. The presentation visibly identifies at least a Burmese textbook and an English textbook for Grade 6. This confirms a historical publication milestone and two named Grade 6 textbook examples, not the full Grade 6 subject list or the current edition.

Source: https://www.moe.gov.mm/sites/default/files/Education_Reform_Four_Years.pdf

The Ministry's FY 2018–2019 Annual Performance Review Report includes a programme action to develop and distribute textbooks and teacher-guide books for all grades and conduct new-curriculum training. This is broad historical programme evidence; it does not enumerate current grade-by-grade titles.

Source: https://www.moe.gov.mm/sites/default/files/2018-2019_APR_ENG_0.pdf

A 2026 Myanmar Digital News article says the 2026–2027 basic-education academic year began on 1 June 2026 and describes KG–Grade 12 as being in the fourth year of new-curriculum implementation. This is useful current-year context, not a subject catalogue.

Source: https://mdn.gov.mm/en/importance-basic-education-and-school-enrolment-myanmar

## Publishing checklist for each resource

Before showing a resource as available:

- Confirm grade and subject from an official source.
- Record title, publisher, source URL and publication/curriculum year if stated.
- Check whether the link is an official download or a third-party mirror.
- Record license / permission status separately from public accessibility.
- Do not upload, rehost, sell, or alter official textbook files unless permission or a suitable license allows it.
- Clearly label original THARi learning materials as supplementary content, not official textbooks.
- Add a review date and a reviewer before marking the item verified-current.
