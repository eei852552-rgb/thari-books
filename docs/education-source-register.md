# THARi BOOKS — Education Source Register

Last checked: 2026-11-14

This register separates what the available sources actually confirm from what still needs checking. It is a research aid, not an official syllabus.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current Ministry of Education / Department of Myanmar Examinations publications and official resource pages.
2. Official curriculum-framework documents with a clearly stated version.
3. Reputable education platforms only as discovery aids; verify their claims against official publications before publishing.
4. Older news articles, reposts and third-party file mirrors are historical clues only.

## Confirmed from official Department of Myanmar Examinations pages

### Kindergarten (KG)

The Department's "New Curriculum KG Text Book and Teacher Guide" page lists these resources:

- KG-1 Language Guide
- KG-2 Learning Guide
- KG-3 Teacher Guide

These are listed as KG learning/teacher-guide resources. Do not automatically present all three as student textbooks.

Source: https://www.myanmarexam.org/news/23

### Grade 1

The Department's KG+12 new-curriculum Grade 1 notice lists:

- မြန်မာဖတ်စာ (Myanmar Reader)
- အင်္ဂလိပ်စာ (English)
- သင်္ချာ (Mathematics)

This is the currently confirmed subject/resource list from the official notice reviewed for this register. Other subject names found in older reposts or general school-supply lists are not added as confirmed on the basis of those sources alone.

Source: https://www.myanmarexam.org/news2679.html?page=1

## Discovery-only sources (not sufficient to confirm current subject lists)

- Education for Myanmar's textbook and teacher-guide index lists grade pages from KG through Grade 12. Use it to discover possible resource files, then verify each item against an official source and rights conditions: https://edu4mm.com/text-books/
- The Ministry of Education secondary textbook / teacher-guide development guideline dated September 2017 includes indicative subject families for Grades 6–12. It is historical and includes "TBC" estimates; do not use it as the current definitive syllabus: https://www.lextutor.ca/myanmar/3/SES/Guidelines_for_TB_TG.pdf
- Myanmar Digital News explains the KG+12 5+4+3 structure. This is useful for grade grouping, not a current subject list: https://www.mdn.gov.mm/en/roundtable-revising-new-basic-education-curriculum

## Publishing checklist for each resource

Before showing a resource as available:

- Confirm grade and subject from an official source.
- Record title, publisher, source URL and publication/curriculum year if stated.
- Check whether the link is an official download or a third-party mirror.
- Record license / permission status separately from public accessibility.
- Do not upload, rehost, sell, or alter official textbook files unless permission or a suitable license allows it.
- Clearly label original THARi learning materials as supplementary content, not official textbooks.
- Add a review date and a reviewer before marking the item verified-current.
