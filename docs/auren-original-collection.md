# AUREN — THARi BOOKS Original Collection (50 Books)

## Author identity
- Pen name: **AUREN** (အော်ရန်)
- Publisher / library: THARi BOOKS
- Collection: 50 original works, additional to the existing 10-book catalogue
- Languages: Burmese and English
- Audience: general readers and young learners; clear, age-appropriate writing
- Editorial promise: every title has a distinct subject, central question, narrative approach, examples, and takeaways. Reusing a title or disguising one book as another is not acceptable.

## Non-negotiable manuscript standard
A title is not complete merely because its cover, synopsis, chapter headings, or sample pages exist. A completed manuscript must contain:
1. A full title page, author credit, copyright/originality note, foreword, introduction, contents, and conclusion.
2. Complete prose in every listed chapter, with a coherent beginning, development, and close. No placeholder text, repeated filler, or chapter summaries presented as chapters.
3. Burmese and English editions. The English edition must be a genuine authored edition, not a machine-like word-for-word rendering; the Burmese edition must read naturally and preserve the same meaning.
4. Specific examples, case studies or original illustrative stories, practical applications where relevant, and reflective questions.
5. A source and fact-check note for factual claims. Clearly distinguish established evidence, interpretation, and fictional examples. Do not invent quotations or citations.
6. Original illustrations or diagrams where they materially improve understanding, with descriptive alt text and captions.
7. A final editorial pass for spelling, grammar, consistency, factual accuracy, repeated passages, chapter order, translation alignment, and age-appropriate presentation.

## Rights and originality
- The 50 new works are original AUREN manuscripts, not adaptations or translations of copyrighted books.
- Any quotations, facts, or historical/scientific claims must be properly attributed and checked. Keep quotations short and within applicable rights.
- Do not reproduce existing authors' distinctive expression, plots, characters, or chapter structures.
- Existing classic books keep their original authorship credits; AUREN is credited only for the new original collection and for clearly identified THARi-created editorial material.

## Production and acceptance workflow
- Keep the existing 10 titles separate; do not overwrite their author credits or content.
- Create each new title as its own manuscript and catalogue record. Reserve a unique stable ID and unique cover/illustration assets for each title.
- Draft the full Burmese manuscript, then produce and align the English edition.
- Run editorial, factual, originality, and technical checks before marking a manuscript complete.
- Only add a book to the public reader when its complete manuscript and required metadata are ready. Do not label a planning document or partial draft as a finished book.
- Keep changes on a dedicated working branch and leave production deployment/merge for explicit owner approval.
